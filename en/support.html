Questions that come up

How do I delete my account?
Inside the app: Profile → App Settings → Delete Account. The account and all its data are removed permanently, and you do not need to contact us to do it.
The analysis gave me a number that does not match my meal.
The numbers are estimates. You can log the meal manually with values you know, and save it to your library to reuse in one tap.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel there; access continues to the end of the paid period.
I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password?” and a link is sent to your email.
What do you keep of my data?
Set out in detail in the privacy policy, and nothing beyond it.
